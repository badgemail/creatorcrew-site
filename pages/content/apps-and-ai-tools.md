# Current Apps & AI Tools

*The Creator Starter Kit: Content* teaches skills that don't go out of date. The apps it uses do change — features move, free tiers shift, new tools arrive. This page is where that gets kept current. Everything here is **as at the date at the bottom of the page**.

## Editing

**CapCut** — the main editor the book teaches (Chapter 6): cuts, captions, music, transitions, and built-in AI tools.

- Opens, edits and exports without an account, and a free export doesn't carry a watermark.
- Free exports in full HD (1080p) are limited to a set number each month, so don't count on unlimited HD.
- The desktop version works the same way. If a sign-in window pops up, close it — no account needed.
- After exporting, save the video to your device. The share buttons that pop up afterwards lead to platforms that need accounts.
- CapCut's terms are written for 13 and up, and ask for a parent's okay under 18 — so if you're younger, use it with a parent alongside.

**InShot** — a free alternative covering the same ground.

- No account needed.
- Free exports carry a small InShot watermark in the corner, and an ad plays before exporting.

**VN** — another free alternative.

- No account needed.
- Ads show on the export screen. No watermark in our test — a follow-up check is on the way.
- VN's terms, like CapCut's, start at 13 and ask for a parent to be involved under 18.

## Thumbnails and titles

**Canva** — the ready-made thumbnail templates are sized exactly right.

- Needs a free account (13 and up; often through school). A sign-in comes before the editor opens: you can browse templates without one, but not edit them.
- A Canva account isn't a social media account, and Australia's under-16 rule doesn't cover it.

## AI tools

- **Inside CapCut:** one-click auto-edit that assembles a rough cut, auto-captions, and AI avatars for anyone who'd rather not appear on camera.
- **Dedicated AI voiceover tools such as ElevenLabs:** not day-one gear — CapCut's own voiceover tools cover the basics. If you try one, check the tool's own age rules first and get a parent's okay. One rule matters more than any feature: never clone a real person's voice without their consent.

## For 16 and over, in Australia

**Instagram Edits** — a genuinely good editor, but it needs an Instagram login just to open, so in Australia it's for 16 and over. If that's you and you already have Instagram, it's worth trying alongside CapCut — the skills carry straight across.
