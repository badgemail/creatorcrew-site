Why it exists

Apps change, AI features change, and Australian rules on young people and social media are actively moving right now — faster than any print edition can track. Rather than promise updates the book itself can't deliver, the current, perishable content lives here instead, free, for every reader.

What's included, free with every copy

  • A short monthly email: new apps and AI tools worth knowing about, and any change to the regulatory picture.
  • This companion site — Current Apps & AI Tools and Regulation Watch — kept current between editions.
  • First access to a discount code when a revised edition ships (ebook refreshed roughly every six months; print roughly annually, given reprint costs).
